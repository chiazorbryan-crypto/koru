Read manhwa through liquid glass

Koru is a personal manga and manhwa reader for Windows. Browse Omega Scans and Vortex Scans, keep a library, download chapters for offline reading and make every pixel your own colour.

Download for Windows View source
Windows 10 and 11 · free · unsigned personal build, so Windows may ask before it opens

Your library

Favourites, continue reading, history and a badge when new chapters land.

A reader that disappears

Long strip or page by page, right-to-left, auto next chapter, keyboard controls and ambient light.

Offline downloads

Save single chapters or whole series and read them anywhere, no connection needed.

Every colour is yours

Each colour has its own transparency, plus glass blur, glow, animation speed, fonts and eight starting looks.

Sidebar where you want it

Left, right, top or bottom, floating or attached, and it minimizes out of the way.

One search, both sources

Type once and Koru searches Omega Scans and Vortex Scans side by side.

Good to know: Koru reads each site's public pages for personal use. Paid or locked chapters stay locked. Please support the scan groups on their own sites.
